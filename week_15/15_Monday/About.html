Common Payroll Questions

My paycheck amount looks different than expected.

  • Compare the hours worked with our timesheet.
  • Review your pay rate.
  • Check taxes and benefit deductions.
  • Compare the paycheck with your previous paycheck.

My direct deposit did not arrive.

  • Verify the scheduled pay date.
  • Check the bank account used for direct deposit.
  • Confirm whether a recent banking change was submitted.
  • Contact payroll if the payment still cannot be located.

When Should I Contact Payroll

Contact Payroll when you have a payroll issue that cannot be resolved using the information and resources available in the Payroll Hub.

Examples include:

  • Incorrect hours or pay rate on your paycheck
  • Missing or incorrect payroll deductions
  • Direct deposit problems
  • Questions about tax withholdings
  • Questions about a payroll correction
