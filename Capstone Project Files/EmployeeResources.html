Frequently Asked Questions

Select a question below to view more information.

Why is my paycheck different this pay period?

If your paycheck looks different this pay period, consider whether:

  • Your tax withholding information changed.
  • Your benefits or deductions changed.
  • You worked overtime this pay period.
  • You completed additional or extra work.
How do I change my direct deposit?

Contact Payroll or HR to update your direct deposit information.

When will my W-2 be available?

Your W-2 will be available by the end of January each year.
